What "normal balance" means

Every account has a side where it naturally rests, the side that makes it grow. Assets and expenses lean debit. Liabilities, equity, and revenue lean credit. If an account ever sits on its wrong side, the books are telling you a story worth investigating, like Cash with a credit balance: the till is overdrawn.
